{# Amazon Easy Ship orders: today's picking sheet, and the order list for reconciliation. Asked for: "after the orders are packed and shipped on the portal my warehouse team is able to reconcile the data. the orders which have to be shipped today. item wise weight wise qty totalled. total number of orders of each item and total orders." So the PICKING SHEET is the default view and the order list is second. Five things are deliberate: 1. **Two sections, two physical jobs.** "To pack & ship" needs picking and labelling; "Waiting for pickup" is already boxed and just needs handing over. One combined "not done" list would merge two different mornings' work. 2. **The empty section is still shown.** Nothing is labelled on a normal morning, so that section reads zero — and a section that vanished would look like a bug rather than an empty queue. 3. **Sizes never collapse.** 500g and 1kg of one product are separate lines, or the packer visits the wrong shelf. The KG column is what the courier needs, and it is NET — packaging is not in the catalogue. 4. **Every time is IST.** Amazon sends 18:29Z, which is 23:59 IST — end of day in India. Rendering UTC would show every deadline 5.5 hours early on the one screen whose job is "what must go out today". The server converts; this page never does. 5. **The refresh banner is prominent.** An order shipped in Seller Central leaves the sheet only on the next refresh, so a stale sheet is this feature's main failure mode. Refreshing pages Amazon at one call every 22 seconds, so it is a background job the page polls — not something that can happen on load. #}
